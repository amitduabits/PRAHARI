\section{The problem, in one picture}
Imagine 26 schools in one city. Each school has cameras. Each school bought a different cupboard to store the tapes. Some cupboards keep tapes for 7 days. Some keep them for 15. The head office has a notebook of stolen bicycles and missing children. Nobody in the camera room opens that notebook when a bicycle rolls past.

That is Gujarat's CCTV estate. Twenty-six departments. Analog and IP cameras. Sites from border districts to Valsad, Dahod, Somnath, Jamnagar, and Dwarka. Watchlists already exist: VAHAN, SARTHI, eGujCop, AFIS, NAFIS. The cameras do not match against those lists. There is no statewide census of which public-domain cameras exist, whether they are alive, or where coverage stops.

The official page is \code{https://sentinel.gujarat.gov.in/problems}. Phase 1 closes 07 September 2026 at 12:00 IST. PRAHARI is Category 1 (student). The team is Amit Dua, Arnav, and Aria.

\begin{keyidea}
The State does not need a new cupboard for every tape. It needs one notebook that watches the cameras it already paid for.
\end{keyidea}

The official core goal is a secure, scalable, interoperable, technically feasible, and cost-effective approach that uses existing infrastructure to the maximum practical extent. A student PoC cannot replace 26 VMS contracts. A central recorder that ingests 80{,}000 full-HD streams (DESIGN TARGET) for 15 days needs statewide dark fibre, petabytes of hot storage, and a GPU farm this team does not have. PRAHARI therefore adds an intelligence plane on top of the systems that already record video.

\section{The four official challenges}
The problems page names four hard facts, not four features to demo.

Heterogeneous infrastructure: vendors, VMS products, AMC contracts, analog and IP, H.264 and H.265, RTSP and HLS, 7-day and 15-day retention. A design that assumes one brand dies on day one.

Geographical dispersion of about 1{,}000\,km. A border outpost and a Paldi junction do not share a LAN. UDP RTSP dies across NAT. The integrator guide therefore forces TCP.

Unified analytics and event handling: the same watchlist must fire on a Home camera and on a Transport camera. The event JSON must not change when the bus later becomes Kafka.

Onboarding without a redesign: a new department is a CSV plus a protocol adapter, not a rewrite of \code{analyse()}.

\section{The four official models, and the choice}
The official page lists four reference models and permits a hybrid. Think of them as four ways to organise a school.

\begin{definition}[Model 1: registry and GIS]
A roll call. Who owns the camera, where it sits, whether it is alive, how many days it keeps tape. No live picture is required.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Model 2: unified viewing]
One window that can open many classrooms. The school keeps its own cupboard. The window speaks RTSP, ONVIF, or a vendor API. ANPR may run on a sampled frame without storing every frame.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Model 3: VMS federation]
A post office between cupboards. Each vendor gets an adapter. Events travel on a bus. The original cupboard stays.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Model 4: central VMS]
One giant cupboard for the whole city. Ingest, store, playback, full AI, hot/warm/cold tape. This is a rip-and-replace.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[PRAHARI hybrid]
Model 1 supplies camera metadata, GIS, health, and gap reports. Model 2 opens reachable RTSP, HLS, WHEP, or ONVIF streams and runs analytics on sampled frames. Thin Model 3 is a detection-event JSON bus, an in-memory watchlist matcher, and \code{GET /api/track/\{plate\}}. Model 4 central recording is Phase-2 for selected cameras only.
\end{definition}

A new vendor is one adapter. The detection JSON does not change if the bus later becomes Kafka. Kafka, Kubernetes, and Ceph are named as later swap points. They are not in this PoC.

\section{Registry and GIS (Model 1)}
A roll call needs a row per camera: id, department, ownership, consent, lat, lon, protocol, retention, health. Three doors put a row in: a CSV, a form, a REST call. A fourth door pulls the Sentinel catalogue.

Health is a colour on the Gujarat map. Three failed probes turn a pin red. Dahod GSRTC is seeded offline on purpose so a gap report has something true to say. Food and Civil Supplies cameras with 7-day retention appear in the short-retention list.

Private-permitted mall cameras require \code{consent=true}. A Home department viewer never sees that mall. That is the law of the notebook, not a CSS trick.

The live Sentinel catalogue has no coordinates. Those rows therefore do not sit on the map (the map already skips lat $=$ 0, lon $=$ 0). Operators open \code{cam04} from the Cameras table. The UI states that fact in words.

On 03 September 2026 the live portal is \code{https://cctv.corp8.cloud/}. Catalogue \code{GET /cameras.json}, after password login, returns a JSON array of \code{\{id, name\}}. \section{Live capture and the integrator laws}
Why TCP, not UDP? UDP dies across NAT and most campus firewalls. Partial UDP looks like a broken model. The capture client sets \code{OPENCV\_FFMPEG\_CAPTURE\_OPTIONS=rtsp\_transport;tcp} before it imports OpenCV. If port 8554 is blocked, the client falls back to HLS.

Why PTS, not the wall clock? After connect, the gateway may replay a group of pictures faster than real time. A tracker that stamps arrival time invents impossible speeds. Event time is \code{CAP\_PROP\_POS\_MSEC}. Declared FPS is unused.

Why backoff 2\,s to 30\,s? Feeds restart. A tight reconnect loop is a denial of service against ourselves.

Why a scene cut? Each sandbox feed loops. At the loop the picture jumps. Object track ids and face tracks reset. A PTS rewind or a gap above 5\,s is that jump.

Why a cap of four open captures? A laptop is not a regional GPU site. \code{MAX\_OPEN\_CAPTURES} defaults to 4. The fifth open raises. SCALE\_BENCH MEASURED that fact on 04 September 2026.

\begin{implnote}
\code{tests/test\_integrator\_laws.py} greps the capture client for the TCP flag, forbids \code{CAP\_PROP\_FPS} in services, and forbids publish helpers. \code{test\_scene\_cut.py} checks rewind and gap, and checks that object track ids reset.
\end{implnote}

\section{\code{analyse()}: one door for every engine}
A child should not have four different ``look at this picture'' buttons that disagree. Adults should not either.

\begin{definition}[\code{analyse(frame, camera, pts\_ms)}]
The only worker entry. It returns a list of detection dicts. It does not insert them. The ingest router writes crops, inserts rows, and calls the matcher.
\end{definition}

Ownership \code{Gov} never receives it either. The log line is \code{frs\_refused}. FaceAnalyzer is not constructed on those cameras. Deleting person events after the fact is not the control. The engine is never started.

\section{ANPR: options and the choice}
A number plate is a name tag glued to a car. We need the letters in a standard spelling so \code{GJ 01 AB 1234} and \code{GJ-01-AB-1234} are the same name.

\begin{definition}[Indian plate normaliser]
After stripping spaces and hyphens, the string must match \code{\^{}[A-Z]\{2\}[0-9]\{1,2\}[A-Z]\{1,3\}[0-9]\{3,4\}\$}. Anything else is dropped.
\end{definition}

\code{recognize(frame\_bgr)} returns \code{\{plate, plate\_raw, confidence, crop\}}. Callers never import Tesseract. If the Tesseract binary is missing, the function returns an empty plate. It does not crash.

Operator confirm writes confidence 1.0 and \code{source=operator\_confirm}. A confirm row is never labelled \code{source=anpr}. That honesty is a test, not a slogan.

\section{Crop honesty}
Every persisted detection writes the original JPEG uri twice (legacy field and \code{crop\_uri\_original}). An optional enhanced copy is a cubic upscale of a low-FFT crop, for a human to look at. Auto-match uses original pixels only.

If a face crop is marked \code{is\_ai\_reconstructed=1}, the matcher inserts \code{status=pending\_review} and does not push a CRITICAL WebSocket card from that crop. A reconstructed face is a review item, not an automatic arrest card.

\section{Intrusion on a godown}
Food and Civil Supplies cameras watch a warehouse, not a highway. A person standing in a drawn rectangle is an intrusion.

The ROI lives in \code{cameras.extra\_json}. Overlap of at least 30 percent of the person box with the ROI raises \code{entity\_type=intrusion}, category \code{INTRUSION}, priority CRITICAL. Two hits within 120\,s on the same camera increment a counter. There is no fourth neural net. Intrusion wraps object boxes.

\section{Watchlist, alerts, track}
The notebook is a representative watchlist. It is not a live ministry pipe. Categories include STOLEN, BLACKLIST, OBSERVE, and a person row \code{MISSING\_ASSOCIATE}.

Duplicate suppression: the same entity on the same camera within 120\,s increments \code{counter}. Priority CRITICAL is stolen, wanted, or intrusion. HIGH is blacklist or the person associate. LOW is log.

\section{Next-camera predict and keyword query}
\code{GET /api/predict/\{plate\}} ranks likely next cameras. First it counts historical transitions out of the last camera that saw the plate. If that map is empty, it ranks GIS neighbours by haversine distance. The method field is \code{frequency+distance}. It is not Kalman. It is not Re-ID. The Track tab has a Next cameras button.

\code{POST /api/query} extracts a plate, a camera id, or an object class with regular expressions. The response includes \code{engine: keyword\_rules}. It is not a language model. Slides must not call it NLP.

\section{Scale and cost}
\label{sec:scale}
A ten-year-old version: do not ship every school video to one room. Ship a tiny still once a second, and only from cameras that matter. The wall shows 16 to 64 live tiles. The rest is metadata.

Numbers below mix MEASURED laptop facts with DESIGN TARGET statewide arithmetic.

\begin{example}[Bandwidth arithmetic (DESIGN TARGET)]
Take 45{,}000 public-domain cameras, 1\,fps, 80\,KB JPEG crop (DESIGN TARGET). Bytes per second $= 4.5 \times 10^{4} \times 8 \times 10^{4} = 3.6 \times 10^9$, which is 3.6\,GB/s if every crop flies to one room. Five regions divide that by five: about 720\,MB/s each. The SOC wall still pulls only 16 to 64 live tiles. Metadata events are hundreds of bytes, not an 80\,KB crop.
\end{example}

Assume 45{,}000 public-domain cameras run analytics at 1\,fps with an 80\,KB JPEG crop (DESIGN TARGET). Naive centralisation is $45{,}000 \times 80\,\mathrm{KB/s} \approx 3.6\,\mathrm{GB/s}$. Five regional GPU sites (Ahmedabad, Surat, Rajkot, Vadodara, Bhuj) cut that to about 720\,MB/s per site. Low-connectivity outposts send events only.

MEASURED on 04 September 2026 on this laptop: four open file captures succeed; the fifth is rejected. Mean JPEG from eight own-feed stills is 41\,KB. \code{/api/health} p99 is 9.4\,ms over 50 calls. GPU count is 0. Statewide 80\,KB and regional GPUs remain DESIGN TARGET.

Order-of-magnitude yearly cost of the intelligence plane, not of replacing 26 VMS estates (DESIGN TARGET): GPU nodes 1.8--2.4 Cr, object storage 0.8--1.2 Cr, bandwidth 0.6--1.0 Cr, twelve engineers 1.5 Cr; total about 5--6 Cr.

Hot / warm / cold recording of every frame is Model 4 and Phase-2, selected cameras only.

\section{Capture internals}
\code{StreamSession} is the only object that holds an OpenCV capture. The TCP transport flag is set in the environment before OpenCV is imported. Open counts against \code{MAX\_OPEN\_CAPTURES} (default 4). The fifth \code{open()} raises. That is a laptop limit, not a statewide SLO.

\code{read()} returns \code{(ok, frame, pts\_ms)}. \code{pts\_ms} comes from \code{CAP\_PROP\_POS\_MSEC}. Wall clock is used only inside \code{backoff\_sleep}. A decoder warning at join is logged. It does not abort the session. HLS origin is pinned to the configured Sentinel host. File protocol is for \code{own\_feed.mp4} on \code{CAM-OWN-001}.

\section{Catalogue client}
\code{app/services/catalogue.py} logs into the Sentinel host with a session cookie, then \code{GET /cameras.json}. Ids come from JSON \code{id} fields. The live host has no coordinates and no \code{/api/ingest}. A missing \code{SENTINEL\_HOST} is valid: the PoC boots on sample cameras. Tests assert 400 when a sync is requested without a host.

\section{Matcher worked example}
Boot loads \code{WL-001} plate \code{GJ01AB1234}, STOLEN, CRITICAL. A detection on \code{CAM-VAL-001} at 06:12 inserts alert $A$ with \code{counter=1}. A second detection on the same camera 10\,s later increments $A$ to 2. A detection 3 minutes later inserts alert $B$. A detection of \code{XX99YY9999} inserts nothing.

A person detection with \code{face\_id=WL-004} and empty plate inserts a HIGH person alert. A reconstructed face with \code{is\_ai\_reconstructed=1} inserts \code{pending\_review} and does not notify CRITICAL.
