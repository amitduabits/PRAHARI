## Category and prizes (Phase 1 sandbox)

Student / small-medium startup is Category 1. Top 3 per category advance. Category 1: ₹4L / ₹2L / ₹1L. Consolation ₹25,000 × 4 across both categories.

Phase 2 (six finalists, no category split): ₹16L / ₹8L / ₹7L, plus ₹50,000 consolation for the other three finalists and a ₹50,000 special jury award.

## Feed-problem reports

When a Sentinel feed misbehaves, report: camera id, exact URL, client and version, UTC timestamp, client-side error log. Confirm `live` in `/api/ingest` before reporting it as down.
