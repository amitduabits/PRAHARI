## WHAT NOT TO DO

### ❌ **Don't:**
1. **Skip literature review.** Don't start writing without knowing what prior work does.
2. **Defer reproducibility.** Don't promise "we'll clean up code after submission."
3. **Hide negative results.** Don't run 10 experiments and report only the 5 that worked.
4. **Overstated claims.** Don't say "proves" when you mean "supports." Don't claim 99% accuracy without measuring.
5. **Write alone.** Don't skip team review. Get Circle A/B/C eyes on every section.
6. **Ignore the promptbook.** Don't "wing it." Follow prompts sequentially.
7. **Ignore timeline.** Don't spend 16 weeks on Paper 1. Keep to 12; Paper 1 is longest.

### ✅ **Do:**
1. **Search literature first.** Before writing, know the landscape.
2. **Code first.** Implement algorithm before describing it.
3. **Honest experiments.** Run all ablations; report all results.
4. **Quantify claims.** Every claim in paper must be backed by number (not "significant," say "24% improvement").
5. **Team review at every step.** Share each prompt output with team.
6. **Follow the promptbook.** It's tried; it works.
7. **Stick to timeline.** 12 papers in 24 months = 2 papers/month on average. Doable if disciplined.

## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

### **Q: Can we do this faster than 24 months?**
A: Probably not without sacrificing quality. Tier-1 venues (CVPR/ICCV) have 6-month review cycles. If rejected, resubmit elsewhere = another 6 months. 24 months is realistic for 6 papers with ≥50% acceptance rate.

### **Q: What if a paper is rejected?**
A: Happens. Do Prompt 6.1 (anticipate objections); address them; resubmit to same venue (revision) or different venue (new submission). Budget an extra 2–4 months for rejections.

### **Q: Should we open-source PRAHARI?**
A: Not until after papers are published. Once first 2–3 papers are accepted, open-source core code. Protects IP while building credibility.

### **Q: Who leads Paper 1?**
A: Circle A lead (Principal Researcher). But this is Hari Om Bansal + team project. Hari Om is co-author on all 6 papers.

### **Q: What if PRAHARI deployment is delayed?**
A: Use mock data for Papers 1–4. By Paper 5, hopefully deployment data is available. Papers that depend on real traces (3, 5, 6) can be adjusted to use synthetic data (less ideal, but viable).

### **Q: How do we handle reviewers who know us / want to de-anonymize?**
A: Venue policy; follow it. If single-blind, be prepared for recognition. If double-blind, anonymize (no self-citations in Paper 1; cite as "[anonymous patent]").
