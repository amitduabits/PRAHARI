# P5 submission checklist (IEEE TCSVT / IVC)

- [ ] Geometric derivation **deleted**, not repaired
- [ ] Schema and collapse predicate presented as background, with IDMEF, OCSF, JDL and Dataflow cited
- [ ] Debar-Wespi cited before page 3
- [ ] W fitted by at least two of the four methods, with agreement or disagreement reported
- [ ] Kneedle run on the operating curve; knee and sensitivity S reported
- [ ] Cost-weighted ROC operating point, with the cost ratio stated explicitly
- [ ] Per-camera fitted W distribution reported; global W kept as a baseline with its cost
- [ ] Window semantics (anchored vs merging) stated precisely and matched to the deployment
- [ ] Case-grouping hypothesis checked against the deployment and reported either way
- [ ] Both failure modes reported for every method: redundant alerts and masked incidents
- [ ] Valdes-Skinner weighted-similarity baseline implemented
- [ ] Operator-burden metric (alerts per operator per hour) reported
- [ ] Real detections with operator-marked incident ground truth, or the synthetic status stated in the abstract
