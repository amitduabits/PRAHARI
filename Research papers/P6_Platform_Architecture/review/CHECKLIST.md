# P6 submission checklist (IEEE TETC)

- [ ] One falsifiable design claim stated, with the conditions under which our choice is wrong
- [ ] Franklin, Wiederhold, Lenzerini and NGSI-LD cited before page 3
- [ ] At least one external baseline measured (NGSI-LD registration, ONVIF auto-onboard, or VMS federation)
- [ ] Transport **failure** distribution measured and reported, not just the mix
- [ ] Scale knee located: what fails at 8,000, what changed for 80,000
- [ ] Three invariants stated, with a measured consequence for dropping each
- [ ] Onboarding constants reported as distributions with sample sizes, not means
- [ ] Retention policy derived from the audit-growth measurement, and fed into the ethics section
- [ ] Audit-log guarantee described honestly as evidentiary, with CT and PeerReview as the stronger alternatives
- [ ] Ethics section is mechanism, not citation; `fussey2021assisted` engaged
- [ ] Modelled versus measured stated per figure, in the caption
- [ ] Design describable without the deployment — check by deleting the deployment name and re-reading section 4
