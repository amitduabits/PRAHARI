# Limitations (template)

Sources, in order: section (B) of `literature/REVIEW.md` (what is anticipated),
section (D) where present (what a reviewer will demand), and the "not run" and
"refuted" rows of the claim-to-evidence table.

The section must contain at least one item that costs us something. A limitations
section made only of future work is a warning sign to reviewers.

Structure:

1. **What the evidence does not cover.** Synthetic versus real data, single
   deployment, single estate, modelled versus measured — stated per claim, not in
   general.
2. **What the method does not do.** The threat, workload or regime outside scope,
   named precisely, with the work that does cover it cited.
3. **Where a baseline wins.** Name it, give the number, explain why we still
   prefer our design, or concede that for that operating point we do not.
4. **What we could not verify.** References we could not confirm, measurements we
   could not take, and assumptions we asserted rather than tested.

Write it in our own words before a reviewer writes it in theirs.
