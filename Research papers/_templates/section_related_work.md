# Related work (template)

Write this section **first**, from `literature/matrix.md`, before the method or the
evaluation. Three paragraphs, in this order.

**Paragraph 1 — what we are reproducing.** Name the works that own the mechanism,
in the first two sentences. Not "several works have considered X" but "X was
introduced by [a] and generalised by [b]". If the mechanism is not ours, this
paragraph says so and the reader stops worrying about it.

**Paragraph 2 — the axes.** Walk the columns of the matrix. For each closest work,
one sentence on what it does and one on the property it lacks. No adjectives.

**Paragraph 3 — the gap.** One sentence, the same one as in `matrix.md`, stated as
what has not been done rather than as what we are the first to do. Then one sentence
conceding the strongest objection, in our words.

Rules:

- The five works in section (A) of `literature/REVIEW.md` are all cited before page 3.
- Every citation has been read at least as far as its method section.
- No work is characterised more weakly than its authors would.
- If our contribution is combinational, the word "combination" appears here.
