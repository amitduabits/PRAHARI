# Phases and official prize structure

Source: https://sentinel.gujarat.gov.in/phases  
Archived for PRAHARI: 2026-08-31  
Grand total: ₹51,00,000

## Phase 1 — Sandbox (₹18,00,000)

Teams integrate with test feeds. Two categories. Top 3 per category receive Phase 1 prizes and become the six finalists. Phase 1 prize money is also a grant to refine the finale solution.

| Position | Category 1 (students, small & medium startups) | Category 2 (large startups & companies) |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | ₹4,00,000 | ₹5,00,000 |
| 2nd | ₹2,00,000 | ₹3,00,000 |
| 3rd | ₹1,00,000 | ₹2,00,000 |
| Total | ₹7,00,000 | ₹10,00,000 |

Consolation: ₹25,000 × 4 next-best teams across both categories (₹1,00,000).

PRAHARI registers in **Category 1**.

## Phase 2 — Production / Grand Finale (₹31,00,000)

Six finalists integrate with real CCTV feeds at scale, irrespective of category. Evaluated by Gujarat Police leadership and a technical jury.

| Position | Prize |
|---|---|
| 1st (Grand Winner) | ₹16,00,000 |
| 2nd | ₹8,00,000 |
| 3rd | ₹7,00,000 |

Additional at production round (₹2,00,000): ₹50,000 consolation × 3 remaining finalists, plus ₹50,000 special jury award.

## Dataset claims on the home page

30+ cameras (evaluation page says ~50), 12 hours of footage per camera, live simulation pipeline. Five departments named on the home page: Health, Police, GSRTC, Panchayat, Municipal. Problem statement additionally names Home, Food & Civil Supplies, RTO. PRAHARI registry must accept any department string.
